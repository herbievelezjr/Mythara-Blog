# Witnessed news.

Every story below was read by eight independent witnesses — not to tell you what to think, but to show you the gap between **projected intent** (what the actors say they're doing) and **shadow intent** (what the evidence suggests they're actually doing).

When the witnesses disagree, the disagreement is published. When the evidence is thin, the witnesses abstain rather than guess. Every claim traces back to the article it came from.

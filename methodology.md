# How the witnessing works

## The pipeline

1. **Ingest.** A bot pulls real RSS feeds — wire services, outlets across the spectrum, Denver local news. Every article is stored with its URL, outlet, and fetch time.
2. **Cluster.** Articles about the same event are grouped together, so one event is seen through every outlet that covered it.
3. **Evidence.** Each event gets a structured evidence pack: the factual claims (each tagged to its source), direct quotes with attribution, named actors — and a framing section showing how each outlet headlined the same event.
4. **Witness.** Eight independent witnesses read the evidence. Each engaged witness writes two readings: the **projected intent** (what the actors and the framing claim is happening) and the **shadow intent** (what the evidence pattern suggests is actually happening), naming the evidence it leans on.
5. **Dossier.** The readings are published with dissent intact.

## The eight witnesses

Each witness reads from its own domain — power, duality, communication, consequence, and the rest. A witness **abstains** when its domain isn't engaged by the evidence. Abstention is published, not hidden. Silence is information.

## The honesty contract

- **Nothing is invented.** Every claim in a dossier traces to a fetched article. If the evidence is thin, witnesses abstain instead of guessing.
- **Dissent is preserved.** Witnesses are never averaged into one verdict. Disagreement is the product.
- **The record is chained.** Evidence and judgments are hash-chained, so the dossier you read is the dossier that was witnessed — unaltered. The chain proves the record wasn't changed. It doesn't prove the record was true. That's what the witnesses are for.
- **This is analysis, not verdict.** The network shows you the gap between what's projected and what's in shadow. What you conclude is yours.

# How this gets made

1. **We pull the feeds.** Real RSS feeds — wire services, outlets across the spectrum, Denver local news. Every article is saved with its link, its outlet, and when we fetched it.
2. **We group the stories.** Articles about the same event go together, so you see one event through every outlet that covered it.
3. **We lay out the evidence.** The key claims (each linked to its source), direct quotes with names attached, and how each outlet headlined the same event.
4. **Eight reporters read it.** Each one writes down what the coverage claims — and what they actually notice in the evidence, with links to what they leaned on.
5. **We name the shadow intent.** For every story, the reporters point at what's happening, name the hidden motive the coverage is serving, and say what it means for real people — who gets helped and who gets hurt.
6. **We print the disagreement.** The readings go up with dissent intact.

## The eight reporters

Each reporter has a beat — framing, follow-the-money, what's missing, and the rest. When a story doesn't touch their beat, they sit it out. We print that too. A quiet reporter is information.

## The rules we follow

- **Nothing is made up.** Every claim links to the article it came from. Thin evidence means quiet reporters, not guesses.
- **Facts and inference stay separate.** What's sourced from an article and what's a reporter's reading are never mixed. We never imply we know something we didn't publish.
- **Punch up, never down.** The lens aims at the seats of power — public figures, institutions, the people making the decisions. Never private citizens.
- **Disagreement stays on the page.** We don't average eight readings into one. The argument is the point.
- **The record is locked.** Evidence and readings are hash-chained, so the page you read is the page that was written — unaltered. That proves nobody changed it after the fact. It doesn't prove the reporting was true. That's what the reporters are for.
- **This is reading, not ruling.** We show you the coverage, the shadow intent behind it, and what it means for real people. What you conclude is yours.

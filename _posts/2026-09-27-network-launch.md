The Mythara News Network opens today with a simple premise: the news tells you what happened, but the gap between what's **projected** and what's in **shadow** is where the real story lives.

Every dossier published here is read by eight independent witnesses. Each one writes two readings — the projected intent (what the actors say they're doing) and the shadow intent (what the evidence suggests they're actually doing). When they disagree, you'll see the disagreement. When the evidence is thin, they'll abstain rather than guess.

No invented sources. No averaged verdicts. Every claim traces to the article it came from.
